# Blue Pill — long-form look (DRAFT, not locked)

Clean SaaS-explainer look: light ice-blue fields, saturated blue gradients, everything rounded into pills and tiles. **Opt-in BY NAME only**, never default. This doc locks only after the look ships on a real job.

References: [`reference/`](reference/) (4 frames).

## The look, read off the references

- **Palette:** ice-blue / off-white field (#EEF4FD, #F4F4F4), one saturated blue (#1E7BF2 → #3B82F6) as the accent and gradient end, black type, a green (#4CD964) only for a "done" state.
- **Type:** heavy geometric sans, tight tracking, black headline with ONE word or line in accent blue. Big numerals in blue for numbered beats.
- **Shapes:** pill cards with a thin white stroke and soft shadow; rounded-square icon tiles, solid white glyphs on blue, slightly rotated and floating; vertical gradient bars as backdrop rhythm.
- **Depth:** soft radial blue glows, frosted glass panels, concentric orbit rings.

## Cards in the set

1. **Numbered section title:** big blue numeral, two-line title, icon pill above, gradient bars framing.
2. **Headline + floating tiles:** centred headline, accent-blue keyword, four icon tiles in the corners, glow dome below.
3. **Notification card:** app-style task card (icon, meta line, title, two pill buttons) over an orbit scene.
4. **Numbered step pills:** stacked grey pills with blue numerals, glass side rail with icons, on a blue gradient field.
